## Theorem
In triangle $A B C$,
$$
a/(sin A) = b/(sin B) = c/(sin C) = 2R
$$

## Special cases
Recall that in a right triangle, $2R$ is simply the length of the hypotenuse, and plugging this in to the formula above recovers the $"opposite"/"hypotenuse"$ definition of sine.
