title: On ego and mortality
draft: true
---
"Lost in my head" may be a cliche phrase, but I feel it's what most accurately describes my pre-2025 persona: so infatuated with my own mind that I reduced the rest of my body to an accessory. Only after an onslaught of physical struggles did I finally begin to understand my true fragility, a revelation which also moved me to replace the pretenses in my self-image with authenticity.
