---
title: Euler line
---
We wish to show that for any triangle, $H G = 2G O$. To do this, notice that we can interpolate between the altitudes and perpendicular bisectors to get the median. Let $l$ be the line parallel to $h_A "and" perp_(B C)$ that passes through $G$. Then notice that this is $2/3$ of the way from $h_A$ to $perp_(B  C)$.  Therefore, $G$ is $2/3$ of the way from $H$ to $O$, completing our proof.
