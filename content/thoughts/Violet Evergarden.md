## Initial impressions

### 1

Pretty beautiful first episode. I like that all the characters feel complex and was especially surprised by Violet's interaction with the Evergarden mother. She seems overly nice at first, but Violet shows us that this hospitality is actually due to her desire for Violet to replace her dead son. Hodgins also seems to have interesting motives, so I'm eager to learn more about him too.

Also, the last bit of this episode was great and really makes me want to keep watching. To think Violet had been holding on to those words for so long and is only finally beginning her journey to understand what they mean...
