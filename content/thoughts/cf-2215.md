## C. Oriented Journey

we direct the edges such that $u -> v$, where $u < v$, denotes that $s_u$ and $s_v$ are different, and otherwise same. since there's only $n - 1$ provided bits, we can fix $s_n = 0$. thus we can root our tree at $n$ and deduce all the bits from there.
