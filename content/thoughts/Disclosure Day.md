## Um...what?

I don't have much to say about this movie. It raised mildly interesting questions without ever answering them, refused to confront the actual interesting aspect of Disclosure Day (how people might react to it), and also has one of the most confounding plots I've ever seen—I mean, those magic wands were some of the worst deus ex machina I've ever seen. Pretty much everything [Pitch Meeting](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AEOTI6959nM) has to say about it.
