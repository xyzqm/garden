this is probably one of the most creative [[akari]] puzzles i've done so far!

the entire puzzle pretty much repeats the same central shape, so let's just analyze this shape first:
![[Pasted image 20260416175507.png]]
note that to light the entire green region, we must either light *two diagonally adjacent squares (e.g. the red)* or the *central square (i.e. the blue)*.

here, we can't light the two red squares because they are both adjacent a single 1. in fact, for this specific green region, any lighting schema with two lights will conflict with one of the 1 tiles. therefore, the blue light **must** be lit.

![[Pasted image 20260416175850.png]]
after placing this light, note that in order to light all 4 green regions, we must either choose all the red lights or all the blue lights. in either case, the 4 green Xs are eliminated.
