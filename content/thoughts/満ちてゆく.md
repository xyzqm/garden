走り出した午後 **も** / even the afternoons that began to run
重ね合う日々 **も** / even the days we shared together

> [!note]- も
> The phrase 「A も」essentially means "even with A/in spite of A..." 
> 
> *Ex.* いつも means "always" but literally いつ (when) + も = "no matter when".

**避けがたく** 全て終わりが来る / unavoidably for all, an end comes

> [!note]- 難い（がたい）
> 避け + がたい = avoid + hard to = unavoidable (ex. 避け難い 真実 = **unavoidable** truth).
> In the song, replacing the い with く switches from adj. to adv. (unavoidable to unavoidably).
> *Similar suffixes:* たい (避け たい = want to avoid), 易い (避け 易い = easy to avoid)
